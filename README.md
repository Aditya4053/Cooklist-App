Cooklist-App — Mise
A daily cooking planner that turns a budget and a few preferences into a full day's meal plan, grocery list, ingredient substitutions, and a budget feasibility check.
What it does
Meal plan — AI-generated breakfast, lunch, and dinner based on your budget, household size, diet, and cuisine preference
Cooking to-do list — short prep/cook tasks for the day, tagged by meal, checkable as you go
Grocery list — a receipt-style shopping list with quantities, estimated prices, and a running total
Substitutions — swaps for ingredients that are expensive, hard to find, or off-limits, with the reasoning
Budget feasibility — a spend-vs-budget gauge, a suggested per-meal budget split, and a note on what to adjust if you're over
Saved days — every plan is saved automatically and can be reopened, checked off, or deleted later
All data is stored locally to your session — no separate backend or database required.
How to use it
Open `mise-cooking-planner.html` (see live link below, or download and open it in any browser)
Enter your budget, household size, diet, cuisine, and anything already in your pantry
Click Build today's plan
Work through the to-do list and grocery checklist as you shop and cook

Tech
Single-file HTML/CSS/JS app. Meal plans, grocery lists, and budget breakdowns are generated by calling the Claude API directly from the browser.
Note
Prices and quantities are AI-estimated planning guides based on typical market rates — they'll vary by store, season, and location.
